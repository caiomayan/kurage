# Legal and privacy readiness

**Status:** drafts implemented; professional review and controller details are
pending. **Last validated:** 29 Aug 2026.

Routes `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy`, and `/legal/acceptable-use` replace the
footer's inert links. They describe only the current product, state that Maré is
not for sale, and give the virtual inventory no financial value.

Before accepting real users, the owner must provide the controller's legal
identity, tax/company details when applicable, address, privacy channel, and
support channel; map processors and international transfers; approve legal bases
and retention; implement data-subject requests; version and record acceptance;
and obtain review by a qualified Brazilian professional.

This engineering material is not legal advice. Normative references include the
[LGPD](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm),
[ANPD guidance on data-subject rights](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/titular-de-dados-1),
and its [official guide for processing agents](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/nova-versao-do-guia-dos-agentes-de-tratamento).
Payments add consumer law, cancellation, refunds, recurring billing, and tax
document obligations to the release gate.
